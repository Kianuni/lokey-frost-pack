The learned LNLM v2 target-embedding residuals (scripts/lnlm/target-deltas.tsv,
also included in lokey_lexicon.bin) were adapted in 2026 by the Lokey project
using Universal Dependencies Chinese GSDSimp v2.16, derived from UD Chinese GSD.
The residuals are provided under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International. Changes include extracting simplified Chinese word contexts,
mining competitors and fitting and quantizing pairwise target-embedding updates.
No treebank text is distributed with the keyboard.
